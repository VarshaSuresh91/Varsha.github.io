Research / Formal methods & software assurance

Reasoning about
software correctness.

My research focuses on testing and verification of real-time embedded software, with broader interests in dependable financial systems and trustworthy AI.

01 / Research foundations

01

Formal verification

Precise specifications and rigorous reasoning about program behaviour, with an emphasis on software correctness.

02

Symbolic execution & testing

Exploring execution paths, generating useful tests, and understanding the trade-offs between coverage and analysis cost.

03

Real-time & embedded systems

Analysing reactive and periodic programs in which timing and repeated interaction with the environment matter.

02 / Ongoing directions

Questions I am
investigating.

Cost-aware test generation and selection

Investigating how symbolic execution and test selection can reduce execution or replay costs while retaining coverage of embedded reactive programs.

Themes: coverage preservation · cost models · test selection

Explanation consistency in NLP

Studying the robustness of explanations for text classifiers under meaning-preserving perturbations, considering both explanation similarity and prediction stability.

Themes: explainable AI · robustness · experimental evaluation

Analysis of periodic and PLC programs

Exploring data-flow and abstract-interpretation techniques for understanding how values and relationships evolve across program cycles.

Themes: abstract interpretation · periodic execution · PLC software
